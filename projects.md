## Flying Hand: Aerial Robotic Manipulation
**Carnegie Mellon University | AIR Lab**

Currently developing a fully-actuated aerial robot designed to perform precise non-destructive testing (NDT) for industrial assets. 

* **Platform Development:** Building a custom fully-actuated multirotor capable of exerting 6-DoF wrenches while maintaining stable hover, enabling direct physical interaction with the environment.
* **Control & State Estimation:** Implementing robust fly-by-wire interaction controllers and precise state estimation pipelines to handle external disturbances during contact-based inspection tasks.

## Commercial Cargo UAVs: Hyperlaunch & Hypercopter
**F-drones | UAV Systems Engineer**

Spearheaded the end-to-end development and commercial deployment of two MVP autonomous cargo delivery drones, handling everything from hardware integration to flight testing.

* **Autonomy & Flight Control:** Led the autopilot development and sensor fusion tuning. Integrated RTK/dual-GPS setups to ensure precision navigation even in environments with high magnetic interference.
* **Computer Vision Integration:** Developed and deployed a precision autonomous landing system utilizing static H-Markers. This significantly increased the VTOL autonomous landing accuracy on moving vessels.
* **Systems Architecture:** Managed version control and collaboration entirely through GitHub, streamlining the workflow for custom Ground Control Software development and flight log analysis.
* **Testing & Validation:** Conducted extensive HITL/SITL simulations and over 100 hours of experimental flight testing to validate control laws and ensure safe commercial deployment.

## The Skytroopers: Heavy-Lift Fixed-Wing UAVs
**SAE Aero Design | MIT World Peace University**

Led a 30-member multidisciplinary team as Captain and Mechanical Design Head to design, manufacture, and fly heavy-lift RC aircraft for international competition.

* **Mechanical Design & Simulation:** Extensively utilized SolidWorks for CAD, Finite Element Analysis (FEA), and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) to optimize the aerodynamic efficiency and structural integrity of the aircraft.
* **Rapid Prototyping:** Managed the end-to-end fabrication process, utilizing laser cutting, 3D printing, and composite materials to build robust, lightweight airframes.
* **Leadership & Operations:** Oversaw team operations, budget management, and successfully secured sponsorships, ultimately winning the "Outstanding Technical Presentation" award at the Asia-Pacific level.
